5. Rules on the Use of Cookies

The website lolaacoustix.com uses cookies. Cookies are text files that the browser stores on the computer system. Many cookies contain a so-called cookie ID — a unique cookie identifier that allows websites and servers to distinguish individual browsers from other browsers carrying different cookies.

Through the use of cookies, the website provides users with more accessible services that would not be possible without cookies. The website's information and offers can be optimised through cookies so that the user and their user experience come first.

The User can prevent the placement of cookies at any time by adjusting the settings of the browser they use, thereby permanently disabling the placement of cookies. Cookies that have already been set can be removed at any time through any browser. However, if the User disables cookie placement in their browser, they will not be able to use all the functions of the website.

You can find more detailed information about the types of cookies we use on our Cookie Policy page.
