8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access (Art. 15) — confirmation of whether we process your data, and a copy of it.
  • Rectification (Art. 16) — correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) — deletion of your data, e.g. once it is no longer needed or you withdraw consent.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) — limiting how we use your data in certain situations, e.g. while you contest its accuracy or while it is unclear whether our grounds override yours following an objection.
  • Data portability (Art. 20) — receiving data you provided us in a structured, machine-readable format, or having it transmitted directly to another controller, where technically feasible.
  • Objection (Art. 21) — at any time, for reasons relating to your particular situation, you may object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), including related profiling. We will stop processing your data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)) — where any part of our processing relies on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time for the future, without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out. The contact form itself is based on Article 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f), not on consent.
  • Lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77) — in the event of a GDPR breach, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged breach. 10. Cookies

Websites generally use so-called "cookies" — small files stored on your device either temporarily (session cookies) or permanently (persistent cookies), which can be technically necessary or used to analyse user behaviour and for marketing purposes.

lolaacoustix.com does not currently set any HTTP cookies and does not use analytics, marketing, or third-party cookies. Your browser only stores two small technical entries (locally, not as classic cookies): lolaCookieConsent, which remembers that you have seen the cookie notice, and lolaSection, which remembers which section of the homepage you were last viewing, for smoother navigation — deleted when you close the browser tab. Neither is shared with third parties or used for tracking. See our Cookie Policy for details.

11. Server Log Files

Our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically collects and stores information in so-called server log files, which your browser automatically transmits. Collection of this data is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — we have a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and optimisation of our website, which requires collecting server log files.

When you send us an enquiry via the contact form, your details from the form, including the contact information you provide, are stored with us for the purpose of processing your enquiry and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass this data on without your consent.

Processing of this data is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to the performance of a contract or to taking pre-contractual steps. In all other cases, processing is based on our legitimate interest in efficiently handling enquiries addressed to us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Technical details of our contact form.
