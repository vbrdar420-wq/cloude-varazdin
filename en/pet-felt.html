PET Felt Acoustic Panels

PET felt acoustic panels are a sound-absorbing material made from recycled polyethylene terephthalate (PET) fibres, most often sourced from plastic bottles. They cut noise and improve sound quality across a range of spaces — offices, recording studios, theatres, and public venues.

From plastic bottle to acoustic panel.

Key Benefits

  • Sound Absorption — effectively dampens sound reflections, cutting echo and creating a more comfortable acoustic environment.

  • Sustainability — made from recycled materials, eco-friendly, and helps reduce waste.

  • Adaptability — available in a range of shapes, sizes, colours, and thicknesses, suited to both aesthetic and functional needs.

  • Easy Installation — lightweight and simple to install, suited to both temporary and permanent setups.

  • Durability — resistant to moisture, mould, and fungus, for long-lasting performance.

All in all, PET felt acoustic panels are an effective way to improve sound quality while championing sustainable design.
