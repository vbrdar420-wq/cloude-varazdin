How Our Material Is Made
Our Material

PET Felt — the Eco Heart of Every Product

Every acoustic panel we make starts as PET felt — plastic bottles shredded, melted down, and spun into fibre, then pressed into dense, rigid boards ready for acoustic use. Each bottle gets a second life in your space instead of ending up as waste.

70% Recycled

Made from recycled PET bottles.

Superior Acoustics

Excellent sound absorption, no compromise on quality.

No Harmful Substances

Safe for offices, homes, and public spaces.

The
Lola Story

Lola was never a business plan.

She was a dog.

A loyal companion who reminded us every day that the best things in life are often the simplest ones.

When we started building what would later become Lola Acoustix, we chose her name as a reminder of the values we never wanted to lose — curiosity, loyalty, honesty and joy.

Today, we design spaces where people feel better.

And every time we see the name Lola, we remember why we started.
